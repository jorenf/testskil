FAQ

Common questions

What AI models power StunningAI?

StunningAI uses a curated database of design rules combined with Claude AI for context-aware reasoning, giving you recommendations grounded in real design expertise rather than random generation.

Does it work with my existing codebase?

Yes. StunningAI outputs stack-specific code snippets in your framework of choice (React, Vue, Tailwind, Flutter, etc.) that can be dropped directly into your project.

Are the color palettes WCAG compliant?

All palettes are tested to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text) and are labeled with their compliance level so you can make informed decisions.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel at any time from your dashboard and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
